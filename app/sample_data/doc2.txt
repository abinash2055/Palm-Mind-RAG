Company Interview Guidelines

This document contains information about our interview process.

The interview typically consists of three rounds:
1. Technical screening - focuses on data structures and algorithms
2. System design - evaluates architecture and scalability knowledge
3. Behavioral interview - assesses cultural fit and communication skills

Candidates should prepare by:
- Practicing coding problems on platforms like LeetCode
- Reviewing system design patterns
- Preparing stories about past projects

The interview is usually scheduled for 45-60 minutes per round.
We use video conferencing tools for remote interviews.
